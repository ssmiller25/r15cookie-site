The site theme will be nearly the same. I'll now be maintaining the upstream [PaperCSS](https://github.com/ssmiller25/papercss) theme as well as the [r15-papercss-hugo-theme](https://github.com/ssmiller25/r15-papercss-hugo-theme) repo under my [ssmiller25](https://github.com/ssmiller25/) GitHub account.

LLMs have given me the ability to maintain code that I wouldn't have otherwise considered maintaining. Although not an expert in frontend development, I'm relying on deterministic gates to highlight issues and suggest best practices. Combining conventional deterministic tooling and LLMs expands the individual's ability to keep alive projects they depend on or simply find interesting.

If the site looks identical to you, good. That was the plan.

Bonus was the addition of explicit Dark-Mode toggles, labels "Ink" and "Paper". Inspired by the [Herdr Plugin Page that uses the same terminology](https://herdr.dev/plugins/). The terms were very fitting for a theme that emphasises a hand written feel.
